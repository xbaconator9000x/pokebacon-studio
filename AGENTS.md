# Development continuity

At the start of a PokéBacon development session, read
`docs/feature-audit/README.md`, `ROADMAP.md` and the relevant records in
`inventory.json`. Check current branch, open issues and delivered-source
reconciliation before selecting work. Continue the highest-value unmet
dependency; do not restart completed systems or repeat settled audits.

Use issues #15 (feature delivery), #18 (adapters/reliability), #14 (release
acceptance) and #20 (comprehensive audit) instead of duplicating their work.
Keep planned, implemented, tested, integrated and released distinct. Update
the inventory and acceptance evidence with each substantial batch.

For native features, record the applicable import/inspect/edit/save/reopen/
export/emulator/reimport/validate stages. Unit or preview success is not game
execution. Preserve opaque commands, unknown data and private project content.
Keep GBA work first, DS deferred, and paid usage at zero unless authorised.
