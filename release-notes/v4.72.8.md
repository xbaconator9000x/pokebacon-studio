This one pulls in a lot of work from the last few weeks.

**New**
- Native event trades and scene recipes, plus an event audit that flags problems before you export.
- Trainer party editing that keeps your ROM's original data when you haven't changed the layout.
- ROM hex and typed tables workshop (Engine & source project). Bookmark addresses, search with wildcards, review and revert staged byte changes, and export an IPS patch.

**Fixed**
- The ROM check on older Android WebViews was giving wrong results. It now matches the standard, and your existing projects aren't touched.

**Worth knowing**
- I've tested the update, opening projects and ROM export on my phone. I haven't played a full game through on it yet, so tell me if anything looks off.
- Back up your projects before updating.
- This release is the Android APK only. There's no new iOS build yet.

Found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/pokebacon-studio/issues) with your app version, device and what you were doing.
